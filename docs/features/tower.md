# Tower

Tower owns the runway: it clears you onto it, off it, and onto it again at the other end of the flight.

## Departure

**Line up and wait** when Tower can use the time: never with an arrival close in on your runway.

**Takeoff clearance**, with the wind (gusts and wind shear when there are any) and where to go after takeoff:

> "November five four seven Golf Alpha, wind one six zero at eight, fly heading one six zero, runway one six left, cleared for takeoff."

On an RNAV departure it is "RNAV to" the first fix instead of a heading.

**When you have to wait**, Tower says why, and clears you without being asked once the reason is gone:

| Reason | What you hear |
|---|---|
| Traffic on final | "hold short of runway one six left, traffic on a two mile final" |
| Wake turbulence behind a heavy | "expect departure in two minutes" |
| The runway is occupied | Tower holds you, then clears you once it is clear |

With an arrival a few miles out, the clearance may be "cleared for takeoff, no delay". Waiting at the line with an arrival further out, Tower may ask "are you ready for an immediate departure?"

**Your requests**: an intersection departure, a turn-out or a heading after takeoff, "need a few minutes". Another runway or full length sends you back to Ground. If Tower cannot, it says "unable" with the reason. If *you* say "unable" to a line up and wait or an immediate departure, Tower offers an alternative.

**After takeoff**, about 1,000 ft above the ground: "contact Seattle Departure".

## Arrival

**Landing clearance**, with the wind, your number in the sequence and traffic to follow:

> "November five four seven Golf Alpha, wind one seven zero at one zero, runway one six center, cleared to land."

Read back "cleared to land" with the runway. If the runway is not free yet, Tower says "continue" and clears you once it is.

**Go-arounds.** Tower sends you around when the runway is not clear, and expects one when you call it yourself. You get the missed approach instructions and "contact Approach".

**Lined up for the wrong runway?** "Confirm you are lined up for runway one six center." Close in, it is a go-around.

**The runway exit.** On the rollout: "turn left at Mike, hold short of runway one six left". Read it back. Once you are clear of the runway: "contact Ground".

## Tower calls you

- **A takeoff or landing readback you owe**: "confirm cleared for takeoff runway one six left".
- **Rolling without a clearance**: "stop immediately, I say again, stop immediately".
- **A cancelled takeoff**: at low speed "cancel takeoff clearance" with the reason; faster, "stop immediately".
- **A possible pilot deviation**, when you took off or landed without a clearance or a readback. The window shows a red alert.

## What Tower knows about traffic

Tower clears takeoffs and landings against the other aircraft in your X-Plane: its AI traffic and aircraft a multiplayer plugin adds. A runway with a departure still on it, or an arrival on short final, is not yours yet. Closely spaced parallel runways are treated as one runway for wake turbulence.
